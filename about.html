ABOUT MILA

Beauty,
made your way.

Mila is a modern cosmetics destination created for those who believe beauty should be simple, expressive and personal.

OUR STORY

Beauty should feel
personal.

Mila was created with a simple idea — beauty should never feel like a set of rules. It should be something you explore, enjoy and make your own.

From everyday essentials to products that help you create a little extra glow, Mila brings together beauty products across makeup, skincare, haircare, fragrance, body care and lips.

We believe that getting ready is more than a routine. It is a small moment to pause, express yourself and feel confident in your own skin.

FROM THE FOUNDER

"Beauty is not about
becoming someone else."

Mila began with a love for beauty and the belief that everyone should have the freedom to define beauty in their own way.

I wanted Mila to be more than a place to shop. I wanted it to feel like a space where discovering something new, trying something different and expressing yourself could all be part of the experience.

— Founder, Hepsi
